# CASPR · cyberfunky hardware

Use the character of a compact studio effects pedal: dark green charcoal, soft module outlines, tactile controls, and small playful color accents. Keep text readable and the surface calm.

## Shape and spacing

- Modules use 22 px corners, a 1 px outline, and a faint inner edge, with 12 px settings padding and 14 px message-shell padding.
- Controls use 7–12 px corners and 6–10 px internal padding. A small dark lower edge gives buttons physical depth.
- Use 8–12 px gaps; arrange voice choices in two columns. Keep related controls together inside their module. Keep settings actions in a padded footer outside the scrolling content.
- The message window is a compact 560 px surface: CASPR and voice selector, message entry, then keyboard hints. Enter submits speech. There is no Speak button.

## Type and interaction

Use Adwaita Sans / Noto Sans. Body text is 13 px, help 11 px, section names 15 px, and the settings title 22 px. Reserve wide lettering for the wordmark and short module titles. Use monospace for shortcut labels and volume values.

Use coral, aqua and lime in module headings and selected controls, with neutral body text. Preserve visible keyboard focus and text labels alongside color. Volume has a continuous aqua track, a round hardware-style thumb, and a numeric percentage. Avoid glow, gradients, decorative circuitry, noisy textures, and neon saturation; the contours and proportions carry the style.

Settings apply and persist as controls change; there is no Save button. Voice selection stays synchronized between Settings and the message window. Playback changes affect subsequent playback.
